# OpenWorld Privacy Policy

Effective: October 19, 2026

This policy covers the OpenWorld apps that OneX Engineering ("OneX", "we") publishes for iPhone, Android, Mac, Linux, and Windows.

## In short

OneX collects nothing. OpenWorld has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads, and no crash reports. Your files, the faces in them, and your results stay on your device. OneX never receives them.

## 1. What OneX collects

Nothing. OpenWorld does not send OneX, or anyone else, your files, the faces in them, face templates, results, device identifiers, location, contacts, or usage data. OpenWorld does not use the camera or the microphone, and it does not run in the background.

OneX does not sell or share personal information, and it does not use personal information for advertising.

## 2. What stays on your device

- **The file you choose** stays where it already was. OpenWorld only reads it and never deletes it.
- **A copy of a shared file**, made when the file has no place on disk, is kept in the app's cache. It is deleted when you choose the next file, when you delete its result, or at the next launch.
- **Video frames and pictures being read** are kept in memory only, and dropped after each frame. They are never saved.
- **Face templates** (numbers that describe a face) are kept in memory only, and dropped when the scan has decided that face. They are never saved.
- **A result** (the faces found as small crops, each candidate's frame, and the report) is kept in the app's own data folder. It is deleted when you delete it, or the next time the app opens after 7 days or after its poster set expires, whichever is first.
- **The poster set** (FBI poster photos and their templates) is kept in the app's own data folder. It stops being used after 7 days and is replaced at each update. Deleting the app deletes it.
- **Settings** (the last model bundle and coverage you picked, and the version of these terms you accepted) are kept in the platform's app preferences until you delete the app.

Some of the faces in your files belong to other people. OpenWorld treats them the same way: on your device only, and deleted on the schedule above. See the Biometric Data Policy.

On iPhone, iPad, and Mac, results are excluded from iCloud and device backups, and on iPhone and iPad they are protected with the system's file protection. On Android, OpenWorld turns off app backup. On Linux and Windows, results are kept in your user data folder (`$XDG_DATA_HOME` or `%LOCALAPPDATA%`), protected by your account's file permissions. OpenWorld does not encrypt them itself.

## 3. When OpenWorld uses the internet

A scan never uses the internet. OpenWorld connects to the internet only when you ask it to:

- **Update posters** downloads the public FBI poster list and poster photos from `api.fbi.gov`, `www.fbi.gov`, and `fbi.gov`, over HTTPS. The request names OpenWorld and carries no cookies. It sends nothing about your files. These FBI sites see your device's internet address, as any website does.
- **Open FBI page** opens one poster's page on fbi.gov in your browser. Your browser, and the FBI's website, then work under their own privacy policies.

The policies and licenses are built into the app, so reading them uses no network. The FBI's handling of a visit to its sites is described in the FBI's own privacy policy, not this one.

## 4. App stores and your device

Apple, Google, and other stores collect data when you download or update an app, under their own policies. OneX does not receive that data beyond the aggregate download reports the stores give every developer. Your device's operating system may also keep its own records. OneX does not control either.

## 5. Children

OpenWorld is for adults. It does not knowingly receive data from anyone, including children, because it receives no data at all.

## 6. Your choices and rights

You can delete any result in the app, and deleting the app deletes all of its data. Because OneX holds no personal information about you, there is nothing for OneX to look up, correct, delete, or hand over when you ask. You may still contact us with any question.

California residents: OneX does not sell or share personal information, and does not collect the categories the CCPA describes. Texas residents: OneX does not capture or receive biometric identifiers; face templates are made and kept only on your device (see the Biometric Data Policy). Washington residents: see the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

## 7. Security

OpenWorld relies on your device's own security for what it keeps there. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and OneX cannot guarantee the security of your device, your operating system, or the FBI's websites.

## 8. Changes

If OpenWorld's data handling changes, this policy changes first, in the same release, and the new effective date is shown here. A change that sends any data off your device would be shown in the app, and you would be asked to accept it, before it takes effect.
