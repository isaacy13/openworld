## 2. What OpenWorld does and does not do

OpenWorld finds faces in a file you choose and compares each one, on your device, with the photos on public FBI posters. When a face passes a locked cutoff, OpenWorld shows a possible candidate and a link to that poster's FBI page.

- **It is not an identification.** Look-alikes, relatives, and old photos can pass the cutoff. The app shows how many false candidates chance alone gives your scan. How often it misidentifies people of different ages, sexes, and skin tones is not measured yet.
- **It is not a clearance.** It misses many real matches, especially when the poster photo is old or the face is turned. A face that is too small, blurred, or turned is not compared at all.
- **It does not report.** OpenWorld does not contact the FBI or any agency for you. Opening a poster visits fbi.gov in your browser. Whether you contact an agency is your decision.
- **It is not authenticated.** OpenWorld does not check that a file is genuine, unedited, or as old as it says.
- **The posters are the FBI's.** They are copied from fbi.gov as published. OneX does not write, check, or correct them.
