# OpenWorld Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective: October 19, 2026

Washington's My Health My Data Act asks some apps to publish this policy. OneX Engineering ("OneX") publishes it for OpenWorld so that what the app does is clear.

- **Consumer health data collected:** none. OpenWorld computes face templates on your device to compare faces with FBI posters. It never sends them, or anything else, to OneX or anyone else (Biometric Data Policy).
- **Purpose:** none, since nothing is collected.
- **Sources:** none.
- **Shared with:** no one. OneX does not share or sell consumer health data.
- **Third parties and affiliates it is shared with:** none.
- **Your rights:** you may ask OneX to confirm whether it collects, shares, or sells your consumer health data, and to delete it. The answer is that OneX holds none. To delete what OpenWorld keeps on your device, delete a result in the app or delete the app (Privacy Policy, section 6).

To make a request or ask a question, email support@onexengineering.com.
